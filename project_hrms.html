Overview

NEXHR is an enterprise-ready, AI-powered Workforce Intelligence and Human Resource Management monorepo platform designed for small-to-medium businesses. Built to replace fragmented HR spreadsheets and disconnected legacy portals, it unifies live attendance tracking with GPS geofencing, role-based employee lifecycle directories, automated payroll calculation, and an intelligent AI Copilot that detects workforce attrition risks and payroll discrepancies in real time.

Monorepo Architecture & Key Modules

NEXHR AI Copilot

Natural language analytics assistant analyzing workforce retention scores, leave spikes, and predictive shift efficiency models.

Attendance & GPS Geofence Engine

System-verified punch-in / punch-out recording with real-time shift duration countdown and coordinate validation.

Multi-Tenant Role-Based Access Control

Strict hierarchical permission matrix separating `SUPER_ADMIN`, `COMPANY_ADMIN`, and `EMPLOYEE` portals.

Automated Payroll & Invoicing

Dynamic salary ledger accounting for tax deductions, overtime rates, and Razorpay subscription billing.

Immutable Audit Trails

Cryptographically logged administrative events for compliance, security reviews, and employee record integrity.
