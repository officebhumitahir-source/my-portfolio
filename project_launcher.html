Overview

In an age of constant notification dopamine spikes, the Minimalist Launcher is an engineered digital antidote. It strips away seductive bright app badges, casino-style micro-animations, and infinite scrolling feeds, replacing the interface with pure typographic intent.

Built around the "Function over Friction" philosophy, this launcher forces intentionality into every phone interaction. Users reclaim hours of their daily focus by breaking compulsive device wake-ups.

Engineered Modules
Text-Only App Drawer
No graphical icons to trigger dopamine responses. Instant fuzzy search.
Configurable Focus Timer
Block time-sink apps during deep study or high-priority engineering sessions.
Notification Batching
Collect incoming alerts silently and deliver them only at scheduled windows.
Ultra-Light Footprint
Weighs under 2.5 MB. Zero background battery drain or tracking telemetry.
