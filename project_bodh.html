Overview

Bodh (बोध) is a privacy-first, local-only Android application engineered to help individuals break dopamine-fueled scrolling habits and reclaim daily focus through intentional friction. Unlike mainstream analytics apps that harvest behavioral data, Bodh operates on an uncompromising zero-network policy: the application does not request the Android `INTERNET` permission, guaranteeing that 100% of telemetry, hourly usage breakdowns, and application habits never leave the physical device.

Engineering Modules

Native UsageStats Telemetry

Integrates directly with Android's system-level `UsageStatsManager` to record exact foreground application duration and hourly distribution.

Mindful Friction Gates

Custom configurable pause intervals (e.g. 10-second breath gates) before opening high-dopamine social apps.

24-Hour Hourly Histogram

Granular hourly bar graphs displaying usage spikes, peak distraction intervals, and day-over-day focus deltas.

Awareness Streaks & Focus Tiers

Milestone challenges (Seeker of Calm, Mindful Observer, Habit Challenger, Focus Guardian, Bodh Master) rewarding digital discipline.

[SACRED_USER_PRIVACY]

Built with local Room SQLite architecture. Absolutely zero ad trackers, analytical SDKs, or cloud dependencies. 100% of telemetry stays on your hardware.
