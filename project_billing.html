Overview

A robust, high-throughput retail billing and inventory management system designed specifically for small-to-medium storefronts and warehouses. Engineered around rapid checkout lanes, it emphasizes keyboard-first shortcuts, lightning-fast item lookups, and clear ledger accountability.

By integrating automatic stock deduction and client credit logs, cashiers can complete transactions in seconds while managers maintain real-time inventory visibility.

Core Capabilities
Instant Printable Bills
Direct rendering of thermal receipts (58mm/80mm) and standard A4 commercial tax invoices with QR codes.
Live Stock Deduction
Atomic database transactions ensure zero stock desynchronization during peak rush-hour sales.
Customer Ledger & Credit
Full khata ledger tracking customer debts, partial payments, and credit histories with timestamped receipts.
Offline Resilience
Keeps billing queue operational even during internet interruptions, automatically reconciling upon reconnect.
