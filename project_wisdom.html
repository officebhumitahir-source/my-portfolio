Wisdom Widget_

Android • Widget • Mindfulness

Overview

A minimalist Android home screen widget that serves a single purpose: delivering a daily dose of wisdom. Whether it's a stoic quote or a Sanskrit shlok, the widget updates once every 24 hours to encourage deep reflection rather than endless consumption.

Key Features

  • 0-Config Installation.
  • Low Battery Usage (Updates via AlarmManager).
  • Offline Database of 1000+ curated quotes.
