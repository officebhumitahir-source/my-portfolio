Legacy Space_

Android • AI • Second Brain

Overview

Legacy Space is your personal digital archivist. It's an intelligent productivity hub that passively captures important screen content and audio, organizes it, and makes it searchable.

Powered by on-device ML and the Gemini API, it turns raw captures into summarized, actionable knowledge, effectively functioning as a "Second Brain" for your mobile life.

Key Features

  • Smart Screen & Audio Capture.
  • On-Device OCR & Text Recognition.
  • AI Summarization via Gemini.
  • Offline-First Privacy.
